# Additional guidance for the AI guide (not on the website)

## Booking lead time
- Bookings require at least 2 days advance notice. Same-day and next-day (tomorrow) bookings are not accepted by the booking system.
- The earliest you can book is 2 days from today (the day after tomorrow), subject to availability.
- Even if you can see a date on the calendar, availability is limited — check the booking widget for real-time availability.

## Start time and meeting
- Start times are flexible and adjusted to your train schedule; tours start between 7:00 AM and 2:00 PM.
- The earlier the better — morning hours tend to offer clearer views of Mt. Fuji.
- Meeting point: Shin-Fuji Station exit. Your guide will wait right at the gate exit with a "Fuji Soul Tours" sign.

## Your guide
- Local-born, Fuji-native, bilingual English speaker with 10+ years of local expertise.
- Philosophy: show you the Mt. Fuji that locals know and love, not just what's in guidebooks and viral reels. No tourist traps, only places we'd take our own family.

## What's included in the private tour price
- Private tour for your group only (van 1–5 guests; private mini bus 6–15 guests, October–December 2026)
- Premium air-conditioned van with luggage storage
- Local-born English-speaking guide
- Bottled water (one per person)
- Hidden photo spots — curated viewpoints locals love
- Luggage-friendly — large suitcases welcome
- Not included: entry fees, food, drinks and activity costs at each stop are paid separately on-site. Every destination and experience card on the website shows its cost — many destinations are free.

## Photo spots we may visit (exact locations are kept private)
- Campsite café near Mt. Fuji (Fumotoppara) — 30–60 min — stunning view of Mt. Fuji through big windows in a campsite café (reservation required)
- Panoramic bridge view — 15–30 min — elevated Mt. Fuji view near the station, perfect for arrival photos
- Plateau view with cows — 15–45 min — up-close dramatic Fuji panorama with cows
- Lakeside viewing spot — 20–45 min — Mt. Fuji reflected on still waters, the "Double Fuji" mirror image
- Convenience-store shots (FamilyMart / 7-Eleven with Mt. Fuji) — 10–20 min — the iconic convenience store with a perfect Mt. Fuji backdrop
- Highland park viewpoint — 15–30 min — peaceful local park with unobstructed Fuji views, away from tourist crowds
- Local craft beer taproom — 20–60 min — small-batch craft beers brewed with Mt. Fuji spring water and organic local grains (weekends only)
- We keep our curated spots private — it's the core value of the crowd-free experience. Exact locations are shared only with booked guests so these hidden gems stay uncrowded. We can share general descriptions of what you'll see, but not specific addresses or map pins.

## Cash
- Bring Japanese Yen cash. Some spots are cash-only — lockers, kimono rental, shrine offerings, certain cafés. Most restaurants and shops also accept cards.

## Shinkansen tickets
- Book Shinkansen tickets via SmartEX (https://smart-ex.jp/en/index.php) — the only official Shinkansen booking site, with the best change and cancellation terms. All other sites are third-party. If the SmartEX mobile app cannot be downloaded on your device, use the web browser instead. You can also buy at the station, but advance booking guarantees your seat.
- Klook and other third-party platforms often don't list Shin-Fuji Station because only the Kodama stops there (not Nozomi or Hikari). This is a platform limitation, not a problem with the station — Shin-Fuji is a fully operational JR Tokaido Shinkansen station. Use SmartEX or buy at any JR station.
- From central Tokyo, take a taxi or train to Tokyo Station or Shinagawa Station (Shinagawa is about 13 minutes by taxi from central Tokyo), then ride the Kodama about 1 hour to Shin-Fuji.

## Hotel pickup
- We cannot pick you up from your hotel in Tokyo. The tour starts and ends at Shin-Fuji Station only. The bullet train takes about 1 hour from Tokyo, versus 2.5+ hours by car.

## Weather, delays and Mt. Fuji visibility
- If the weather is bad or your train is delayed, we have backup plans with alternative cultural experiences and offer flexible rescheduling. Real-time communication via WhatsApp.
- Even when Mt. Fuji hides in the clouds, cultural experiences make the tour rewarding. Your guide adjusts the route to maximize your experience regardless of visibility.

## Children and infants
- Japanese law requires a child safety seat for children aged 5 and under. Depending on the date and number of guests, a child seat can be arranged (rental fee $50 USD). Infants count as a passenger but do not need a separate booking — book for the number of adults only. Please contact us with your date, group size and child's age so we can check.

## Customising the tour
- You can add or swap destinations and experiences based on your interests and timing. After booking, share your preferences and we'll tailor the itinerary.
- After you book, your guide reaches out via WhatsApp or email to (1) confirm your train schedule, (2) ask about your activity preferences, (3) create a personalised itinerary. The itinerary can be revised until tour day.
- If your wish list won't fit in 4 hours, your guide will create an itinerary showing the extra time needed, and you can decide to either trim activities or pay for an extension.

## Reviews
- Reviews are on TripAdvisor and Viator, rated 5.0 stars. Search for "Fuji Soul Tours" on either platform.
